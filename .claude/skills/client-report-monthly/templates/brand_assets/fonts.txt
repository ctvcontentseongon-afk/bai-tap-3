# SEONGON Brand Fonts

## Heading Font
Montserrat Bold (700)

## Body Font
Open Sans Regular (400)

## Fallback Fonts
Arial Bold (headings)
Arial (body)

## Font Sizes
- Slide title: 32pt
- Section heading: 24pt
- Body text: 16pt
- Caption/label: 12pt
- Footnote: 10pt
